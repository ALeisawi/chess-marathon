# Chess Marathon

A mobile chess runner. Start as a pawn and race up a 7 × 1000 board. Black answers every move with a real chess move, and the board collapses behind you.

Play: https://aleisawi.github.io/chess-marathon/ (add it to your home screen for full screen).

- Tap a highlighted square to move. Red squares can be taken by black.
- Take a piece to become it. Every 10th rank is gold and makes you a queen.
- Get taken and you drop one level (queen, rook, knight, pawn). As a pawn you lose a heart.
- Keep moving: the board falls away behind you.
- Four themes and settings for sound, vibration and pop-up messages.
- When a new version is published, the app shows an "Update" banner.
